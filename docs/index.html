How scores are computed
  • Per set: corpus WER and CER, meaning total errors over total reference words or characters, after the same normalization is applied to references and hypotheses (Arabic: diacritics, alef/hamza/ta-marbuta spellings and punctuation are unified and digits are written out as words).
  • Per track: the unweighted mean over that track's sets.
  • Arena: .
  • Uncertainty: 95% intervals from a paired bootstrap over utterances (1000 resamples per set, the same resample for every model). The rank range is how far a model's Arena rank moves across resamples; models whose ranges overlap are not reliably separated.
  • Telephony: 8 kHz, a 300–3400 Hz band, a G.711, GSM, AMR-NB, G.726 or Opus codec, and packet loss. Call-center adds babble, office or street noise and reverb at the caller before that channel.
  • Decoding: greedy, language forced to the suite language, batch inference on one RTX 5070 Ti (16 GB).
  • Models: open-weight models are ranked, all run locally; closed APIs and private-weight models are listed as unranked references.
